Spanish consulates ask for foreign documents to be translated into Spanish. A sworn translation (traducción jurada) is made and signed by a translator appointed by Spain's Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MAEC), and Spanish authorities accept it as an official translation.

I'm Elisa Vílchez, MAEC sworn translator no. 6774. Most of my work is translating documents for Spanish visa and residence applications from the US and the UK.

The usual order

  1. Get the original document (for example, your FBI check or a birth certificate).
  2. Get it apostilled in the country that issued it, if your consulate requires it.
  3. Get the sworn translation into Spanish. Send me a clear scan and I'll send you a quote and a delivery date.
  4. Submit the original, the apostille and the sworn translation with your application.

Each consulate publishes its own requirements, and they differ in the details. I link to the official pages I've checked on each guide.
