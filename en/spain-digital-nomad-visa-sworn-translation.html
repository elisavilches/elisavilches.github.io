For Spain's digital nomad visa, the documents that usually need an apostille and a translation into Spanish are your criminal record check, your company's registry certificate, your degree or proof of experience, and certificates for family members. Your employer letter must be in Spanish or translated.

I'm Elisa Vílchez, MAEC sworn translator no. 6774. Under Spanish law, sworn translations are official translations, so every consulate accepts them.

Two ways to apply

  • From your country, at the Spanish consulate: a telework visa valid for up to one year.
  • From Spain, if you're there legally: a residence authorisation from the Large Companies and Strategic Groups Unit (UGE), valid for up to three years.

The translation rules below come from the consulates. If you apply from Spain, check the UGE's current requirements.

Translation rules

  • The apostille doesn't need to be translated (New York, Washington). The Edinburgh checklist asks for documents to be apostilled first and translated afterwards.
  • Who can translate: US consulates accept sworn or certified translators, and Miami asks for a sworn translator for the criminal record check. In the UK, Edinburgh asks for a sworn translator registered in Spain.
  • Electronic signature: the MAEC allows sworn translations to be signed electronically. I can deliver them that way or on paper.

Income requirement

200% of Spain's minimum wage (SMI). The Washington consulate gives the 2026 figure: €2,442 a month, plus €916 for the first family member and €306 for each additional one.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use a translation done in the US?

Several US consulates accept certified translations. If you plan to apply from Spain or use the documents later at Spanish offices, a sworn translation is the safe choice, because it's the official one under Spanish law.

Does my contract need a sworn translation?

Washington asks for the employer letter in Spanish or translated, without requiring a sworn translation. Check your consulate's page, and ask me if you're unsure.
