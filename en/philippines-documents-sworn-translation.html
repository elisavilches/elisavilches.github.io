If you're applying for a Spanish visa or residence permit from the Philippines, you'll usually need your PSA certificates (birth, marriage or CENOMAR) or your NBI clearance, with an apostille from the DFA and a sworn translation into Spanish. Philippine documents are usually in English, so they need a translation even though they look easy to read.

I'm Elisa Vílchez, sworn translator for English appointed by Spain's Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MAEC), no. 6774. I translate Philippine civil records and clearances for family reunification, student, work and residence applications.

Key facts

DocumentsPSA birth, marriage and death certificates, the CENOMAR (Certificate of No Marriage Record), and the NBI clearance as your criminal record certificate. For school documents, see the diplomas and transcripts guide.
ApostilleFrom the DFA (Department of Foreign Affairs) Office of Consular Affairs. The Philippines has been part of the Apostille Convention since 14 May 2019, so no embassy legalization is needed for Spain.
Electronic documentsThe DFA now issues electronic apostilles for PSA eCertificates, and the NBI clearance for apostille is the NBI eClearance from the eGovPH app. A digitally signed sworn translation fits well with these electronic documents.
How recentOften required to be recent: many authorities want certificates and clearances issued within the last few months. Check the list for your procedure.
TranslationSworn translation into Spanish of each document. The apostille too, if your consulate or office asks for it.
DeliveryQualified electronic signature or paper with original stamp and signature

Step by step

  1. Order the documents with the apostille in mind. The DFA asks for PSA certificates for apostille to be ordered through PSA Helpline, and for the NBI eClearance from the eGovPH app.
  2. Get the DFA apostille on each document.
  3. Send me the files or clear scans of every page, apostille included. I'll reply with a quote and a delivery date.
  4. I translate the documents (and the apostilles if needed), then sign and stamp the sworn translation.
  5. Submit each document with its apostille and its sworn translation.

Save time: family applications often need several certificates (marriage, a birth certificate for each child, CENOMAR). Send them together: one quote and one delivery for the whole set.

Be prepared: things that sometimes come up

Requirements vary between consulates and offices and change over time, so it's better to go over-prepared:

  • Annotations. Corrections and court decisions appear as annotations on the PSA certificate. They are part of the document and go in the translation, so make sure your copy shows them.
  • Names that don't match. Middle names, maternal surnames and married surnames often differ between the PSA certificate, the NBI clearance and the passport. Tell me about any difference: I translate exactly what each document says, and you may need to explain it.
  • Errors on the certificate. The DFA checks that the PSA QR details match the document. If you spot a mistake, sort it out before the apostille and the translation.
  • Hard-to-read entries. If the PSA copy has unclear entries or erasures, the DFA may ask for the Local Civil Registrar copy as well. Send me both if you have them.
  • Electronic or paper. Ask how your consulate or office wants electronic documents and apostilles submitted (the file, a printout or both), and choose the translation format to match.
  • Documents that are too old by the time of your appointment. Order them close to the date, leaving time for the apostille and the translation.
  • Other countries. If you've lived in other countries, you may need their criminal record certificates too.

Frequently asked questions

My documents are already in English. Do I still need a translation?

Yes. Spanish authorities work in Spanish, so documents in English need a sworn translation into Spanish.

Is your translation accepted in Spain and at Spanish consulates?

Yes. Translations by MAEC-appointed sworn translators are official translations in Spain and at Spanish consulates.

Part of my document is in Filipino. Can you translate it?

I'm a sworn translator for English. If any part of a document is in Filipino or another language, tell me when you ask for a quote and we'll see how to handle it.

Digital or paper? How long does it take?

Both formats are possible. PSA certificates and NBI clearances are short documents, and I'll confirm the delivery date with your quote. See the FAQ on digital signature and delivery times.
