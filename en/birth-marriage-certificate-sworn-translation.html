If your spouse or children are applying for a Spanish visa with you, the consulate asks for proof of the family relationship: a birth or marriage certificate issued by the civil registry, with an apostille and a sworn translation into Spanish.

I'm Elisa Vílchez, sworn translator for English appointed by Spain's Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MAEC), no. 6774. I translate birth and marriage certificates for family members on non-lucrative, digital nomad and student visas.

Key facts

DocumentBirth or marriage certificate issued by the civil registry. Unmarried partners can use a registration as an unmarried couple or other proof of the partnership.
Which copyAn official certified copy from the registry. For children, the full (long-form) birth certificate that shows the parents' names, since that is what proves the relationship.
Apostille (US)From the Secretary of State of the state that issued the certificate. Birth and marriage records are state documents, so the federal apostille used for the FBI check does not apply.
Apostille (UK)From the FCDO Legalisation Office. London asks for the apostille to certify the signature of the official who issued the certificate, not that of a notary or solicitor.
How recentIssued within the 6 months before you apply at the Los Angeles and Miami consulates. London does not set a limit. Check your consulate's page.
TranslationSworn translation into Spanish of the certificate. The apostille too, if your consulate asks for it.
DeliveryQualified electronic signature or paper with original stamp and signature

Step by step

  1. Order a new certified copy from the vital records office (US) or the General Register Office or local register office (UK). If your consulate applies the 6-month rule, an old copy will not do.
  2. Get the apostille on that certified copy: Secretary of State of the issuing state (US) or FCDO (UK).
  3. Send me a clear scan of every page, apostille included. I'll reply with a quote and a delivery date.
  4. I translate the certificate (and the apostille if needed), then sign and stamp the sworn translation.
  5. Submit the certificate with its apostille and my sworn translation with your application.

Save time: if your family has several certificates (marriage, and one birth certificate per child), send them together. One quote and one delivery covers the whole set.

Mistakes that delay applications

  • A certificate that is too old. Los Angeles and Miami want it issued in the last 6 months. Order a fresh copy before getting the apostille.
  • A short-form birth certificate. Some short versions do not show the parents. To prove that a child is yours, the certificate needs both parents' names.
  • An apostille on a notarized photocopy. The apostille has to certify the registry's certified copy, not a copy certified by a notary.
  • A state apostille from the wrong state. It must come from the state that issued the certificate, not the state where you live now.
  • Names that don't match. If a name is spelled differently on the passport and the certificate (a maiden name, a middle name, an accent), tell me. I translate exactly what the document says, and you may need to explain the difference to the consulate.
  • Scans that miss the back or the seals. Embossed seals and stamps on the back are part of the document and go in the translation.

Frequently asked questions

Does the apostille need to be translated too?

It depends on the consulate. London says the apostille doesn't need a translation if one of its languages is Spanish. If yours asks for it, I translate the apostille together with the certificate in the same sworn translation.

Can I use the certificate I already have at home?

Only if it is a certified copy from the registry and, where the 6-month rule applies, recent enough. A souvenir or hospital birth certificate is not an official civil registry document.

Is your translation accepted by Spanish consulates?

Yes. Translations by MAEC-appointed sworn translators are official translations in Spain and at Spanish consulates.

Digital or paper? How long does it take?

Both formats are possible; check what your consulate or BLS office accepts. Birth and marriage certificates are short documents, and I'll confirm the delivery date with your quote. See the general FAQ on digital signature and delivery times.
