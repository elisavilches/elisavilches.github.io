I'm Elisa Vílchez, a sworn translator-interpreter for English (Traductora-Intérprete Jurada) appointed by Spain's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, European Union and Cooperation (MAEC) on 17 July 2008, with appointment no. 6774.

Spanish authorities and consulates accept sworn translations as official translations. I sign and stamp each translation and certify that it is complete and accurate. Most of my clients are English speakers who are moving to Spain and need their documents translated for a visa or residence application.

What I translate

I work mostly on documents for Spanish visa and residence applications:

  • Visas: non-lucrative, digital nomad, student and family reunification.
  • Criminal record checks: FBI Identity History Summary and UK ACRO police certificate.
  • Civil records: birth, marriage and divorce certificates.
  • Financial and employment documents: bank statements, tax returns and employer letters.
  • Medical certificates, academic transcripts and diplomas.

I can deliver sworn translations with a qualified electronic signature, which is valid for online applications, or on paper with my original stamp and signature.
