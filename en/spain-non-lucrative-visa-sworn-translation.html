For Spain's non-lucrative visa, your criminal record check always needs an apostille and a translation into Spanish. Whether your medical certificate, bank statements and family certificates also need translating depends on your consulate. Ask me before you pay for translations you don't need.

I'm Elisa Vílchez, MAEC sworn translator no. 6774. A sworn translation is the official translation under Spanish law, so every consulate accepts it, including the ones that also accept other translations.

Which documents need a translation

DocumentWhat consulates ask for
Criminal record check
FBI · ACRO (UK)		Apostille and translation at every consulate. No older than 6 months. US: federal apostille; state or local checks are not accepted.
Medical certificateNo older than 3 months (Los Angeles: 90 days). US: if you use the consulate's bilingual template, no translation is needed (Washington, Los Angeles). UK: London and Edinburgh ask for an apostille and a translation.
Bank statementsWashington and Boston: no translation needed. San Francisco: foreign documents need an official translation. Period required: 3 months (Washington, Boston, San Francisco) or 12 months (Houston, Edinburgh).
Health insuranceIn Spanish, or with an official or sworn translation (Washington). Houston asks for a certificate in Spanish, which insurers usually provide.
Birth and marriage certificates (family members)Apostille and official translation (New York, Chicago, London, Manchester, Edinburgh). Some consulates set a maximum age for the certificate (Los Angeles and San Francisco: 6 months).

Translation rules by consulate

ConsulateWho can translate · Does the apostille need translating?

EdinburghRegistered sworn translator · No. Apostille first, translation after.

If your consulate accepts certified translations, a sworn one is valid too. If you're not sure, a sworn translation avoids the risk.

Financial requirement

The Ministry asks for 400% of the IPREM for the applicant and 100% for each family member. The Washington consulate gives the 2026 figures: €28,800 a year for the applicant and €7,200 for each family member.

Frequently asked questions

Is a translation done by a US translator valid?

Several US consulates (Washington, Boston, Miami, Chicago, Houston, Los Angeles) accept translations by certified translators. London and Edinburgh ask for sworn translators. If you'll use the same documents later in Spain, for example at the immigration office, a sworn translation is the safe choice.

When should I order the translations?

Once you have the apostilled documents. Leave time before your appointment: the criminal record check must be under 6 months old and the medical certificate under 3 months.
