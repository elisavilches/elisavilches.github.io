For Spain's non-lucrative visa, your criminal record check always needs an apostille and a translation into Spanish. Whether your medical certificate, bank statements and family certificates also need translating depends on your consulate. Ask me before you pay for translations you don't need.

I'm Elisa Vílchez, MAEC sworn translator no. 6774. A sworn translation is the official translation under Spanish law, so every consulate accepts it, including the ones that also accept other translations.

Which documents need a translation

DocumentWhat to expect
Criminal record check
FBI · ACRO (UK)		Always needs an apostille and a translation into Spanish. It is usually required to be no older than 6 months. US: the FBI check with a federal apostille; state or local checks are generally not accepted.
Medical certificateUsually no older than 3 months. Some consulates provide a bilingual template that needs no translation; others ask for an apostille and a translation. Check which one yours uses.
Bank statementsSome consulates accept them in English, others ask for an official translation. The period required also varies (often 3 months, sometimes 12). They can run to many pages, so check before you order a translation.
Health insuranceUsually in Spanish, or with an official translation. Many insurers can issue the certificate in Spanish directly.
Birth and marriage certificates (family members)Usually an apostille and an official translation. Some consulates also want them issued within the last few months. See the birth and marriage certificate guide.

Who can translate, and the apostille

Consulates don't all use the same wording: some accept translations by a certified translator, others ask for an official or sworn translation. A sworn translation is valid at all of them, so if you're not sure, it avoids the risk.

Some consulates also ask for the apostille to be translated, and some want the document apostilled first and translated afterwards. Translating the apostille together with the document avoids a second round.

Financial requirement

The Ministry asks for 400% of the IPREM for the applicant and 100% for each family member. With the 2026 IPREM that is about €28,800 a year for the applicant and €7,200 for each family member. Check the current figure with your consulate.

Frequently asked questions

Is a translation done by a US translator valid?

It depends on the consulate: some accept translations by certified translators, others ask for a sworn translator. If you'll use the same documents later in Spain, for example at the immigration office, a sworn translation is the safe choice.

When should I order the translations?

Once you have the apostilled documents. Leave time before your appointment: the criminal record check must be under 6 months old and the medical certificate under 3 months.
